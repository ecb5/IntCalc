Fact 2.2.1.2.

The right side of equation (2.34) consists of rational functions where the degree of the numerator is one less than the degree of the denominator. Since the numerators are all constants (polynomials of degree zero) and the denominators are all linear (polynomials of degree one) that statement is rather bombastic. But its importance will become clear in Subsection 2.2.2.
