Problem 20.1.1.2 The Volume and Surface Area of a Sphere.

Archimedes showed that the volume and surface area of a sphere is \(2/3\) that of the circumscribed cylinder. He was so proud of this particular result that he requested the symbol be carved in his tombstone after his death.
🔗

(a)

Use Archimedes’ result above to derive the modern formula for the volume of a sphere: \(\text{Volume }=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

(b)

Use Archimedes’ result above to derive the modern formula for the surface area of a sphere: \(\text{Surface Area }=4\pi r^2\text{.}\)
