Theorem 2.2.0.10. \textbf{Theorem. The Limit of a Sum of Two Convergent Sequences is the Sum of their Limits.}.

Suppose we have two convergent sequences \(\left(a_n\right),(b_n)\) with \({\mathop{\mathrm{lim}}_{n\to \infty } a_n\ }=a\) and \({\mathop{\mathrm{lim}}_{n\to \infty } b_n}=b\text{.}\) Then the sequence \((a_n+b_n)\) converges and
\begin{gather*} {\mathop{\mathrm{lim}}_{n\to \infty } (a_n+b_n)}=a+b \end{gather*}
