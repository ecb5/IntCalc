Definition 25.2.0.1. A Sequence of Real Numbers.

A sequence of real numbers, is a function \(a\) from the positive integers to the real numbers. In this case for the positive integer \(n\) we denote \(a(n)\) by \(a_n\) and typically denote the entire sequence by \({\left(a_n\right)}^{\infty }_{n=1}=(a_1,a_2,a_3,\dots )\) or \((a_n)\) for short.
