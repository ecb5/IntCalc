Theorem 2.2.0.11. The Limit of a Product of Two Convergent Sequences is the Product of their Limits.

Suppose we have two convergent sequences \(\left(a_n\right),(b_n)\) with \({\mathop{\mathrm{lim}}_{n\to \infty } a_n}=a\) and \({\mathop{\mathrm{lim}}_{n\to \infty } b_n}=b\text{.}\) Then the sequence \((a_n\cdot b_n)\) converges and
\begin{equation*} {\mathop{\mathrm{lim}}_{n\to \infty } (a_n\cdot b_n)}=a\cdot b \end{equation*}
