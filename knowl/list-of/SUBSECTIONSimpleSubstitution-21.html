  11. \(\displaystyle \int x\sqrt{1-x}\dx{x}\)
    🔗
    There are really only two choices for the substitution, and it is not clear that either will work. It is normal, and very human, to sit and stare at the problem wondering what to do when this happens. Don’t do that. It is normal, but it is also unhelpful. Make a substitution, work out the new integral and hope that it is one you can do. If not, start again and make the other substitution.
    🔗
    But don’t wait for the problem to solve itself. It won’t.
