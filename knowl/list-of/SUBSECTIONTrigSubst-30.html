Problem 19.1.5.9.

We know that \(\int{\frac{1}{1+x^2}}\dx{x}=\inverse{\tan} \left(x\right)+C\text{.}\) What about something like
\begin{equation*} \int\frac{1}{{\left(1+x^2\right)^2}\dx{x}}? \end{equation*}
🔗
Use the substitution \(x=\tan (\theta )\) to show that
\begin{equation*} \int\frac{1}{\left(1+x^2\right)^2} \dx{x}=\int\cos^2 (\theta )\dx{\theta}. \end{equation*}
