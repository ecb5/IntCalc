Problem 19.3.0.2.

Observe that the result in this problem is the same as in Problem 8.12.1.2.
🔗

(a)

🔗
Use a substitution to show that
\begin{equation*} \dfdx{\left(\ln\left(ax\right)\right)}{x}=\frac{1}{x} \end{equation*}
also.
