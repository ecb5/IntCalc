Integration and Initial Value Problems.

You’ve seen the Fundamental Theorem Of Calculus (FTC) before but you may not recognize it in this form. To see what we mean let \(F(x) \) be the function defined in the FTC. Observing that
\begin{align*} \dfdx{F}{x}= F^\prime{}(x) = f(x)\amp{}\text{ and that }\amp{} F(a)=0 \end{align*}
we see that \(F(x)\) is the solution of the Initial Value Problem:
\begin{align} \dfdx{F}{x} = f(x),\amp{}\amp{}F(a)=0.\tag{20.6} \end{align}
