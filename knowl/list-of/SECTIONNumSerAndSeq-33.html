Theorem 25.2.0.12. \textbf{Theorem. The Limit of a Continuous Function Evaluated on a Convergent Sequence.}.

Suppose we have a convergent sequence \(\left(a_n\right)\\)with \({\mathop{\mathrm{lim}}_{n\to \infty } a_n\ }=a\text{.}\) Suppose \(f(x)\) is continuous at \(a\) Then the sequence \(\left(f\left(a_n\right)\right)\) converges and
\begin{gather*} {\mathop{\mathrm{lim}}_{n\to \infty } \left(f\left(a_n\right)\right)\ }=f\left(a\right)=f\left({\mathop{\mathrm{lim}}_{n\to \infty } a_n\ }\right) \end{gather*}
