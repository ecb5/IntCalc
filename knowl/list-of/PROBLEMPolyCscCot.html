Problem 2.1.3.7.

The procedure described in Cornerstone Problem 2.1.3.2 can be modified to provide a method for evaluating integrals where the integrand involves the cosecant and cotangent functions.
🔗
(a)
Find trigonometric identities involving the secant and cotangent functions which are analogous to equations (2.12), (2.13), (2.15), (2.16), (2.14), and (2.17).) Then replace \(\sec\left(x\right)\) and \(\tan\left(x\right)\) with \(\csc\left(x\right)\) and \(\cot\left(x\right)\text{,}\) respectively in parts (b), (c), and (d) of Cornerstone Problem 2.1.3.2 and evaluate the integrals you obtain.
