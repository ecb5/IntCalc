Problem 19.1.3.10.

The procedure described in Cornerstone Problem 19.1.3.8 can be modified to provide a method for evaluating integrals where the integrand involves the cosecant and cotangent functions.
🔗

(a)

Find trigonometric identities involving the secant and cotangent functions which are analogous to equations (19.14), (19.15), (19.17), (19.18), (19.16), and (19.19).
🔗
🔗

(b)

Replace \(\sec\left(x\right)\) and \(\tan\left(x\right)\) with \(\csc\left(x\right)\) and \(\cot\left(x\right)\text{,}\) respectively in parts (b), (c), and (d) of Cornerstone Problem 19.1.3.8 and use your results in part (a) to evaluate the integrals you obtain.
🔗
These integrals appear in applications often enough that you will probably find it useful to commit them both to memory.
🔗
🔗

(c)

In Problem 19.1.4.2 replace \(\sec\left(x\right)\) and \(\tan\left(x\right)\) with \(\csc\left(x\right)\) and \(\cot\left(x\right)\text{,}\) respectively and use your results in part (a) to evaluate all of the integrals that result.
