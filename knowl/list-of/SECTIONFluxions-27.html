Problem 5.8.0.8.

The total revenue gained from selling a certain commodity at time \(t=t_0\) is
\begin{equation*} r(t_0) = p(t_0) \times q(t_0) \end{equation*}
where \(p\) is the price of the commodity and \(q\) is the number of items sold.
🔗
Suppose that at time \(t=t_0\) we know that \(p \) is increasing at a rate of \(5\)% per month and \(q\) is decreasing at a rate of \(3\)% per month. Does this mean that \(r\) will be increasing at a rate of \(5\% - 3\%=2\%\) per month at time \(t=t_0\text{?}\) Explain.
