Example 20.1.1.11.

What is wrong with the following argument?
🔗
We know that the area of a circle with radius \(r\) is \(\pi{}r^2\text{.}\) But we can rearrange the set of all radii of a circle with radius \(r\) into a rectangle with width \(r\) and length \(2\pi{}r\text{,}\) and thus with area \(2\pi{}r^2\text{.}\) Thus by Cavalieri’s Principle \(2\pi{}r^2=\pi{}r^2\text{,}\) or
\begin{gather*} 2=1. \end{gather*}
