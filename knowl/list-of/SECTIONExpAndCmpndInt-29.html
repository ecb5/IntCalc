Problem 8.6.0.6.

An investment \(A(t)\) of one dollar which has a nominal rate of \(5\%\) per year would have the following values if compounded daily and continuously
\begin{equation*} A\left(t\right)={\left(1+\frac{0.05}{365}\right)}^{365t} \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} A\left(t\right)=e^{0.05t} \end{equation*}
What would the difference between the two values of these investments be in \(100\) years?
