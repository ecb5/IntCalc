Example 19.2.3.5.

Our solution to the problem in Example 19.2.3.3 probably appeared to be fairly complex but that is only because we needed to carefully explain our reasoning at each step. Since we don’t need to do that anymore this example will probably seem much simpler, even though it is actually a little bit more complex.
🔗
Consider the function
\begin{gather*} R(x) = \frac{-2x^4+9x^3-7x^2+20x-12}{x^3(x-2)^2}. \end{gather*}
Its decomposition has the form
\begin{align*} R(x)\amp{} = \frac{A}{x}+\frac{B}{x^2}+\frac{C}{x^3}+\frac{D}{x-2}+\frac{E}{(x-2)^2} \end{align*}
From Heaviside’s Method we see immediately that
\begin{gather*} C=\eval{\frac{-2x^4+9x^3-7x^2+20x-12}{(\text{covered})(x-2)^2}}{x}{0}=-3 \end{gather*}
and
\begin{gather*} E=\eval{\frac{-2x^4+9x^3-7x^2+20x-12}{x^3(\text{covered})}}{x}{2}=5. \end{gather*}
🔗
Thus we have
\begin{align*} R(x) \amp{}= \frac{-2x^4+9x^3-7x^2+20x-12}{x^3(x-2)^2}\\ \amp{}=\frac{A}{x}+\frac{B}{x^2}+\frac{-3}{x^3}+\frac{D}{x-2}+\frac{5}{(x-2)^2} \end{align*}
or
\begin{gather} \frac{-2x^4+9x^3-7x^2+20x-12}{x^3(x-2)^2} +\frac{3}{x^3} -\frac{5}{(x-2)^2}= \frac{A}{x}+\frac{B}{x^2}+\frac{D}{x-2}.\tag{19.54} \end{gather}
but the right side of equation (19.54) is the PFD of \(\frac{P(x)}{x^2(x-2)}\text{,}\) where \(P(x)\) is a polynomial to be determined next.
🔗
We now have
\begin{gather*} \frac{-2x^4+9x^3-7x^2+20x-12}{x^3(x-2)^2} +\frac{3}{x^3} -\frac{5}{(x-2)^2}=\frac{P(x)}{x^2(x-2)} \end{gather*}
and we need to determine \(P(x)\text{.}\) Clearing the fractions we have
\begin{gather*} \frac{-2x^4+9x^3-7x^2+20x-12}{x(x-2)} +3\left(\frac{x-2}{x}\right) -5\left(\frac{x^2}{x-2}\right)=P(x). \end{gather*}
🔗
Since we can ignore non-polynomials this becomes
\begin{align*} P(x) \amp{}= \left[-2x^2+5x+3\right] +\left[3\right] -5\left[x+2\right]\\ \amp{}=-2x^2-4. \end{align*}
🔗
Thus we have
\begin{gather*} \frac{-2x^2-4}{x^2(x-2)}=\frac{A}{x}+\frac{B}{x^2}+\frac{D}{x-2} \end{gather*}
from which Heaviside’s Method gives us
\begin{align*} B=\frac{-4}{-2}=2\amp{}\amp{}\text{and}\amp{}\amp{}D=\frac{-2(4)-4}{4}=-3. \end{align*}
Applying Heaviside’s Method one more time we see that
\begin{gather*} A=\CancelTo{-2}{\left[\frac{-2x^2-4}{x(x-2)}\right]}-\CancelTo{0}{\left[\frac{2}{x}\right]}+\CancelTo{3}{\left[\frac{3}{x-2}\right]}=1. \end{gather*}
