Theorem 25.2.0.14. The Limit of a Quotient of Two Convergent Sequences is the Quotient of their Limits.

Suppose we have two convergent sequences \(\left(a_n\right),\ (b_n)\) with \({\mathop{\mathrm{lim}}_{n\to \infty } a_n\ }=a\) and \({\mathop{\mathrm{lim}}_{n\to \infty } b_n\ }=b\text{.}\) Suppose further that \(b,\ b_n\neq 0\) for all \(n\text{.}\) Then the sequence \(\left(\frac{a_n}{b_n}\right)\) converges and
\begin{gather*} {\mathop{\mathrm{lim}}_{n\to \infty } \left(\frac{a_n}{b_n}\right)\ }=\frac{a}{b} \end{gather*}
