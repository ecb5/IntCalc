Theorem 25.2.0.13. The Squeeze Theorem for Sequences.

Suppose we have three sequences \(\left(a_n\right),\left(b_n\right),(c_n)\) with \(a_n\le b_n\le c_n\) for all \(n\) larger than some fixed number \(N\text{.}\) If \(\limit{n}{ \infty}{ a_n }=a=\limit{n}{ \infty}{ c_n }\text{,}\) then the sequence \((b_n)\) converges and
\begin{gather*} \limit{n}{ \infty}{ b_n}=a \end{gather*}
