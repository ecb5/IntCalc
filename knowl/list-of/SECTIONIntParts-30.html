Drill 19.3.0.13.

As we saw in Problem 19.3.0.12 sometimes we have to use the same integration technique more than once. If you perform integration by parts twice, be sure not to switch the roles of “\(u\)” and “\(v\text{.}\)” This is not incorrect, but the second integration will simply undo the first integration and circle back to where you started.
🔗
To see what we mean integrate \(\int x^2\sin(x) \dx{x}\) by parts twice. The first time let \(u=x^2\) and \(\dx{v}=\sin(x)\dx{x}\) and the second time let \(u=\cos(x)\) and \(\dx{v}=x\dx{x}\) to confirm that the second Integration by Parts simply undoes the first.
