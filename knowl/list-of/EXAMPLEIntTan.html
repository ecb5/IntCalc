Example 2.1.3.6. The Integral of the Tangent Function.

Use entry #7 of Table 1.1.0.4 to compute \(\int\tan(x)\dx{x}\) by making the substitution
\begin{gather*} z=\cos(x), \end{gather*}
which means that
\begin{gather*} \dx{z}=-\sin(x)\dx{x} \end{gather*}
or
\begin{gather*} \textcolor{red}{-\dx{z}=\sin(x)\dx{z}}\text{.} \end{gather*}
🔗
Thus
\begin{align*} \int\tan(x)\dx{x} = \int\frac{\textcolor{red}{\sin(x)}}{\cos(x)}\textcolor{red}{\dx{x}} \amp =-\int\frac{\dx{z}}{z}\\ \amp =-\ln\abs{z}+C\\ \amp =(-1)\ln\abs{\cos(x)}+C\\ \amp =\ln\abs{(\cos(x))^{-1}}+C\\ \amp =\ln\abs{\sec(x)}+C\text{.} \end{align*}
